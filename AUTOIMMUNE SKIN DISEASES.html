What are Autoimmune Skin Diseases ?


They are skin conditions that occur due to an immune system disorder,where the body mistakenly attacks its own healthy skin cells.

Vitiligo is a skin condition
Where your skin loses natural

that makes skin cells build up too quickly.

What is Eczema?

Eczema, also called Atopic Dermatitis,
is a chronic inflammatory skin condition.
It happens when the immune system
overreacts and causes redness, dryness,
severe itching, and flaky patches
on the skin.It is not contagious.
